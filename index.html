My name is Yvonne Tran and I am a second-year Biology student student a passion for science and healthcare. I have gained an interest in pursuing a career in dentistry because of its unique blend of patient care and craftsmanship. I enjoy working with people and find it very rewarding when I am able to help patients improve their oral health and regain their confidence.

I am committed to personal growth and am constantly looking for opportunities to gain experiences that will help me prepare for dental school. I have the amazing opportunity of shadowing three general dentist, in which I've learned so much about doctor-patient realtionships and dental procedures. I plan on studying for the DAT over the summer in preparation to apply to dental school.

I'm currently focused on strengthening my academic record, gaining experience, and developing skills for my future. I have explored various health professions, however I have always had an interest in dentistry. I look forward to the journey ahead and am excited about the opportunities ahead in my pursuit of a career in dentistry.
